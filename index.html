How this tool models CPF & projections — please read
  • CPF contribution and allocation rates follow the CPF Board's 2026 age-band schedule (bands: ≤35, 36–45, 46–50, 51–55, 56–60, 61–65, 66–70, 70+). Rates are applied annually, not month-by-month, so figures are planning-grade rather than payroll-exact.
  • Extra interest (+1% below 55 on the first $60,000 combined; +2%/+1% tiered from 55 on the first $60,000) is approximated using CPF's published order of allocation (OA up to $20,000, then SA/RA, then MA).
  • Medisave is capped at the Basic Healthcare Sum. Any contribution or interest that would push MA above it flows instead to SA (below 55) or RA (55 and above), as CPF rules require. BHS is treated as fixed for life once the client turns 65.
  • At age 55, SA closes and transfers fully into a new Retirement Account, topped up from OA up to the Full Retirement Sum you set above. Any OA balance beyond that stays in OA.
  • CPF OA only becomes available for drawdown once the client is 55 or older and their RA has reached the Full Retirement Sum — matching CPF's actual withdrawal rules. Set this up on the Drawdown sequence tab.
  • Risk-on vs. risk-off allocation counts CPF OA and SA/RA, plus liquid cash above the emergency reserve you set, as risk-off. Medisave and the emergency reserve itself are treated as earmarked, not part of the investable allocation.
  • CPF LIFE monthly payout is not calculated by this tool — actuarial payout tables aren't public in a form this tool can model reliably. Enter your client's estimated payout from the official CPF LIFE Estimator into the Cash & CPF LIFE tab.
  • New PRs in their 1st/2nd year (graduated rates) aren't modelled for clients already set to SC/PR — full rates are assumed there. For Employment Pass clients, you can simulate gaining PR status via the field on the Client tab; graduated 1st/2nd-year rates (per CPF Board's published F/G schedule) apply automatically before switching to full rates in year 3.
  • SRS contributions are treated as a cash outflow into a separate pocket that grows at your chosen rate. Once drawdown starts, growth stops and the balance is split into 10 equal yearly withdrawals paid into liquid cash, matching the mandatory 10-year SRS drawdown rule. SRS withdrawals aren't taxed in this tool — add expected tax as a fixed expense if you want to account for it.
  • Existing investments and policies: enter their current value directly rather than trying to simulate years of past history — the tool only projects forward from today.
  • Income, contributions and expenses shift on 1 January of the year they take effect — there's no mid-year proration.
  • No income tax, CPF housing withdrawal, or property value is modelled — add income tax as a fixed yearly expense, and treat property as outside this cash-flow ledger unless you want to add its rental yield or sale proceeds as an income line.
  • When liquid cash would run short in a year, the tool automatically draws down from investments, policies, and (if eligible) CPF OA in the order you set on the Drawdown sequence tab, before letting cash go negative.

Investments
Unit trusts, ETFs, stocks, robo portfolios, SRS investments, endowments held as investment-linked, etc. Dividends can either be reinvested (compounds into the value) or paid out to cash.
Cash-value insurance policies
Whole life, endowment, participating policies. Value grows by your illustrated %, increases with premiums paid, and reduces when coupons or maturity proceeds are paid out. Leave maturity fields blank if the policy simply continues.
